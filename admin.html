Tips for Fast Bulk Uploading
  1. Use hex colour codes from Google "colour picker" — just search "rose gold hex" and copy the # code.
  2. If all products in a group share colours, copy-paste the colour columns down in Excel.
  3. For the SKU, use a pattern like MD-MEN-TI-001 (Brand-Category-Material-Number). Helps you find products fast later.
  4. Set stock to 999 for "always available" products, or the actual count for limited items.
  5. After importing, go to Stock & Sold Out to fine-tune per-colour availability.
Stock & Sold Out
Set stock levels per product and colour. Zero = sold out banner on your site.
How it works: Set the stock number for each colour. When it hits 0, your live site shows "Sold Out" and prevents adding to cart. Below your low-stock threshold it shows a "Only X left" warning. Hit Save after changes.

How to Push Your Changes to GitHub
  1. Make all your edits here in the admin panel and click Save.
  2. Open GitHub Desktop on your PC.
  3. You'll see changed files listed on the left side.
  4. Type a short note in the Summary box e.g. "Added 50 products".
  5. Click "Commit to main" then "Push origin".
  6. Your live site at harshulkumar1.github.io updates in about 1 minute ✓
